FAQ

Membership Questions

Can I change or cancel my plan anytime?+

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel your membership at any time from your client dashboard — changes apply from your next billing cycle.

Do unused sessions roll over to the next month?+

Up to 1 unused session rolls over to the following month. Beyond that, sessions reset with each new billing cycle.

Can I use my sessions on any treatment?+

Absolutely — membership sessions can be used on any massage, body wrap or aromatherapy treatment on our menu, with your therapist of choice.

Is there a joining fee?+

No joining fee on any plan. You only pay the listed monthly membership price.
