FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Send us a message above and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.

How do I book an appointment?+

Browse our treatments menu, pick a service and therapist, and book straight from your client dashboard. You can also call or WhatsApp us using the number above and we'll book it for you.

What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?+

You can reschedule or cancel for free up to 4 hours before your appointment from your dashboard. Cancellations within 4 hours or no-shows may be charged a small fee.

Do you accept walk-ins?+

We're primarily appointment-based to make sure your therapist and room are properly prepared, but we're happy to fit in walk-ins whenever there's a same-day opening — call ahead and we'll check availability.

Is parking available at your location?+

Yes, complimentary parking is available for all clients directly outside the spa on Lotus Garden Road.

How far in advance should I arrive?+

Please arrive 10–15 minutes before your scheduled time so you can settle in, change, and start your treatment relaxed and on time.

Who do I contact about membership or billing questions?+

For anything related to your membership plan or a past payment, email us or call the number above, or check your receipts anytime from your dashboard.
